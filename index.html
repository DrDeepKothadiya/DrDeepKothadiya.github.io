About

Research Expertise

PhD Researcher in Computer Engineering with specialization in Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, and Computer Vision. Currently serving as Assistant Professor at Symbiosis International University (SCIT), and recently completed PhD research at Prince Sultan University, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia (AIDA LAB).

My research focuses on developing interpretable and explainable AI solutions for medical imaging, sign language recognition, and biometric authentication. I am passionate about advancing the transparency of deep learning models through explainable AI (XAI) techniques, ensuring that AI systems are trustworthy and understandable.

Active member of the Editorial Board of Humanities and Social Sciences Communications (Web of Science indexed). Published extensively in top-tier journals and conferences with impact factors ranging from 2.0 to 10.7. Regular reviewer for IEEE, Springer, and other major publishers.
